# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

CouchRemote is an iPhone app and a Mac menu-bar app that let you control your own Mac from your own iPhone. It is built so that nobody, including its developer, receives your data.

## What we collect

Nothing. CouchRemote has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer cannot see what you do in the app.

## What the apps do with your data

- **Local network.** The iPhone and Mac apps talk directly to each other over your Wi-Fi network. Cursor movements, key presses and the text you type are sent only to your paired Mac. They are encrypted with a key that only your iPhone and Mac share, and they never pass through any server.
- **Pairing key.** When you pair, a random key is generated on your Mac and passed to your iPhone through the QR code. Each device keeps it in its own Keychain. It never leaves your devices except through that QR code.
- **Camera.** The iPhone camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code. Nothing is recorded or stored.
- **On-device settings.** The iPhone app stores the names of your paired Macs and a few preferences (such as night mode) on the device. The Mac app stores the names of paired iPhones and its own settings on the Mac.
- **Mac permissions.** The Mac app asks for Accessibility access so it can move the pointer and press keys on your behalf. It uses this only to carry out the commands you send from your paired iPhone.

## Third parties

CouchRemote uses no third-party code, SDKs or services, and shares nothing with anyone.

## Children

CouchRemote does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Everything is stored on your own devices. Use "Forget" on a paired Mac or iPhone in the apps, or delete the apps, to remove it.
